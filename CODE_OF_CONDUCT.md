# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume good faith, respect differing experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and observable behavior. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of private data, and sustained disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, as well as when someone publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

For urgent safety, privacy, or security concerns, use the private reporting channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). For other conduct concerns, contact a listed repository maintainer privately. Include links or screenshots where useful, but minimize collection of personal data.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, with conflicts of interest avoided.
